Every Student Has a Story

At Christian Star Academy, students are known as individuals. We meet them where they are, recognize their strengths and challenges, and help them continue moving forward academically, personally, and spiritually.

An “Academic Hospital”

CSA has sometimes been described as an “academic hospital.” Students may come to us with gaps, frustrations, or simply a need for a different educational approach.
We begin by understanding the student in front of us. We identify where they are, strengthen areas of need, build upon their abilities, and help them progress toward greater independence and mastery.

Known

Our small environment allows teachers to know students personally, recognize their needs, and celebrate their progress.

Challenged

Individualized education does not mean lower expectations. Students are challenged according to their abilities and encouraged to keep growing.

Supported

When students struggle, we work to identify why and provide the instruction, practice, encouragement, and accountability needed to move forward.

Learning, friendships, projects, activities, service, and everyday moments all become part of a student's experience at Christian Star Academy.
