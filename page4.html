THE PEOPLE BEHIND CSA

Our faculty, staff, administration, and Board of Directors share one purpose:
faithfully serving the students and families entrusted to Christian Star Academy.

Miss Sue Feasel has poured her life into faithfully serving Christian Star Academy and the students and families who have walked through its doors. Throughout more than four decades of ministry, she has remained deeply committed to excellence in Christian education and to providing strong, faithful leadership for the school.

Her work extends far beyond administration. Sue has invested personally in generations of students, taking the time to understand their individual strengths, challenges, and needs while encouraging each one toward greater academic achievement, personal responsibility, spiritual growth,
and confidence.

Her dedication to both the daily work of education and the long-term mission of CSA has helped shape the school into the personal, Christ-centered learning community it is today.

Sue Feasel

School Administrator

Mason Qualls

Working closely with students, staff, and administration, Mason is committed to strengthening CSA’s programs, organization, and long-term future while preserving the personal, Christ-centered education at the heart of the school. Mason is passionate about helping CSA grow as a strong and sustainable ministry prepared to serve families for generations to come.

Kathy Bender

As a co-founder of Christian Star Academy, Kathy has been part of CSA’s story from its beginning. Her years of service and continued involvement on the Board provide an important connection between the school’s founding vision and its future. Kathy remains committed to the Christ-centered mission that has guided CSA since 1981 and to seeing that mission continue for generations of students and families.

Cindy Wood

Cindy serves Christian Star Academy as both a Board member and the school’s bookkeeper. Through her careful work behind the scenes, she helps CSA responsibly manage its financial resources and supports the everyday operation of the school. Her service reflects an important part of CSA’s commitment to faithful stewardship, accountability, and providing a strong foundation for continued ministry.

Robert Qualls

Bob brings years of practical service and knowledge of Christian Star Academy to his role on the Board. Having previously served CSA through the care and management of its building, he understands firsthand the work required behind the scenes to support the school’s mission. His continued service helps provide experience, perspective, and a commitment to the long-term stewardship of Christian Star Academy.
