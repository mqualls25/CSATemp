ABOUT CSA

Christian Star Academy is a Christ-centered school committed to providing students with a personal, purposeful education rooted in biblical truth. In partnership with families, we cultivate an environment where students are known, encouraged, challenged, and equipped to grow academically, spiritually, and personally.

Learn More

 

A TRUELY PERSONAL EDUCATION

 

At Christian Star Academy, every student is known as an individual. Our small and personal learning environment gives us the opportunity to understand each student’s strengths, challenges, learning needs, and areas for growth. Rather than expecting every student to follow the exact same path at the exact same pace, we seek to provide the support, encouragement, accountability, and challenge each student needs. Our goal is meaningful growth that prepares students to become capable, responsible, and confident young people.

FAITH AT THE FOUNDATION

 

Christian education at CSA is about far more than adding Bible class to the school day. God’s Word is the foundation for how we approach education, relationships, character, responsibility, and the world around us. We want students to grow in knowledge while also learning to think carefully, make wise decisions, recognize truth, and understand that their abilities have been given to them for a purpose. We seek to prepare students to carry a biblical worldview with them far beyond their years at CSA.

A PARTNERSHIP WITH THE HOME

 

We believe God has given parents the primary responsibility for the education and development of their children. Christian Star Academy exists to come alongside families in that responsibility, not to replace them. Through communication, shared expectations, encouragement, and a common commitment to biblical truth, we work with parents to support the growth of the whole student. When the home and school work together with a shared purpose, students receive a stronger and more consistent foundation for their education, character, faith, and future.
