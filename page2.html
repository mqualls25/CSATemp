INVEST IN CHRISTIAN EDUCATION

For more than four decades, Christian Star Academy has served students and families through personal,
Christ-centered education. The generosity of families, friends, and community supporters helps us continue
that mission while strengthening CSA for the generations to come.

Every gift, large or small, becomes part of providing students with an education
grounded in biblical truth, personal growth, and faithful stewardship.

WAYS TO GIVE

Turn Your Ohio Tax Dollars Into Support for CSA

Through our partnership with the Ohio Christian Education Network Scholarship Granting Organization, Ohio taxpayers have an opportunity to support scholarships for Christian education while receiving a dollar-for-dollar Ohio income tax credit, subject to applicable limits and tax liability.
