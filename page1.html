EDUCATION WITH A PURPOSE

 

Located in Mount Vernon, Ohio, Christian Star Academy has been serving students and families since 1981. CSA began with early childhood classes and a conviction that remains important to our educational philosophy today: a strong foundation in a child’s earliest years can have a lasting impact on future learning, development, and success.

From the beginning, Christian Star Academy has sought to educate the whole student academically, emotionally, physically, spiritually, and socially. More than forty years later, that commitment continues as we provide a personal, Christ-centered educational environment where students can be known, supported, challenged, and encouraged to grow.

Throughout our history, families have come to CSA for many different academic, personal, and spiritual reasons. For some, Christian Star Academy has provided a new beginning when previous educational experiences have been difficult. Our staff has had the privilege of seeing students overcome challenges, discover abilities, develop confidence, grow in their faith, and experience successes that once seemed out of reach. For generations of students and families, CSA has sought to be a safe harbor where they are welcomed, valued, and given opportunities to grow.

We believe education is about far more than academic achievement. It is an opportunity to develop knowledge, wisdom, character, responsibility, and a biblical understanding of the world. God's Word provides the foundation from which we approach learning, relationships, personal responsibility, and the purpose of education itself.

We also remain committed to making Christian education accessible to families whenever possible. Throughout CSA’s history, that commitment has required faithful stewardship, generosity, sacrifice, and trust in God's provision. We recognize that Christian Star Academy ultimately exists to serve His purposes, and we remain grateful for His faithfulness throughout more than four decades of ministry.

Today, we carry that foundation forward with a commitment to strengthen Christian Star Academy for the generations still to come. Our desire is to preserve the personal care, biblical foundation, and whole-student education that have defined CSA since 1981 while continuing to grow into a stronger, more effective, and sustainable school prepared to serve students and families well into the future.

VISION STATEMENT

 

Christian Star Academy will enable parents to continue their God-given role as teachers of their children. Parents will have the opportunity to form a partnership of mutual respect with well-trained, state-certified staff members who are dedicated in their service to the Lord. A God-centered, nurturing, home-like environment will enhance the life-long learning for all those involved. Within this unique environment the most important lessons for all to learn will be what it really means to be a Christian and how we daily make our lives mirror the life of Christ.

Parents and staff will continually seek the Lord's will and His guidance in choosing the correct education plan for each child. This plan will conform to the child and not the child to the plan, allowing for growth to take place in a developmentally appropriate manner.

The fruit of this partnership will be young men and women who will mature to loving God with all their hearts, souls, and minds (Matthew 23:37); who will grow in wisdom, stature, and favor with God and man (Luke 2:52); and who will give sacrificially of themselves and their resources reflecting the essence and the love of Christ who lives and dwells within them (1 Corinthians 10:31; Colossian 3:17, 23).

MISSION STATEMENT

 

In preparing youth for life in the changing society of today, our mission at Christian Star Academy is to support the Christ-centered home as a the foundation for education the whole child as a steward accountable to God. Such an education must be formed upon the absolute authority of God's Word as found in the Scriptures.

STATEMENT OF FAITH

 

1. We believe the Bible to be the inspired, the only infallible, authoritative, inerrant Word of God (2 Timothy 3:15-17; 2 Peter 1:21).

2. We believe there is only one God, eternally existent in three persons-Father, Son, and Holy Spirit (Matthew 28:19).

3. We believe in the deity of Christ, His virgin birth, His sinless life, His miracles, His vicarious and atoning death, His resurrection, His ascension to the right hand of the Father, His personal return in power and glory (John 10:30; 1 Corinthians 15:3-8; Acts 1:11; Revelation 19:11).

4. We believe in the absolute necessity of regeneration by the Holy Spirit for salvation because of the sinfulness of human nature. We believe that people are justified by faith in the shed blood of Christ and that only by God’s grace and through faith we are saved (John 3:16-19; Romans 3:23, 5:8-9; Ephesians 2:8-10; Galatians 3:21-24).

5. We believe in the resurrection of both believers and unbelievers. Those who believe in Jesus Christ are saved to a resurrection of eternal life. Those who do not believe in Christ for their salvation are resurrected to be condemned (John 5:24-30; Revelation 20:15).

6. We believe in the spiritual unity of believers in our Lord Jesus Christ (Romans 8:9; Galatians 3:26-29).

7. We believe in the present ministry of the Holy Spirit who indwells in each Christian and enables him or her to live a godly life (Romans 8:5-17; 1 Corinthians 6:19-20).

8. We believe that the term “marriage” has only one meaning; the uniting of one man and one woman in a single, exclusive union, as defined in Scripture (Gen 2:18-25).

9. We believe that God offers redemption and restoration to all who confess and forsake their sin, seeking His mercy and forgiveness through Jesus Christ (Acts 3:19-21; Rom 10:9-10; 1 Cor 6:9-11).

10. We believe that God wonderfully and firmly creates each person as male and female. These two distinct, complementary genders together reflect the image and nature of God (Gen 1:26-27).

11. We believe that all human life is sacred and created by God in His image. Human life is of inestimable worth in all its dimensions, including pre-born babies, the aged, the physically or mentally challenged, and every other stage or condition from conception through natural death. We are therefore called to defend, protect, and value all human life (Ps 139).

 

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How is Christian Star Academy different from a traditional school?

Christian Star Academy is a state-chartered, non-graded, multi-age, non-denominational Christian school. Rather than placing every student into a traditional grade-level structure and expecting everyone to progress through material at the same pace, CSA focuses on the individual student.

Students work at the level that best fits their current understanding and abilities. This means a student may be working at different levels in different subjects. When a student needs additional time to master a concept, we can provide it. When a student is ready to move forward, learning does not have to stop simply because the rest of a class is not ready.

We use a variety of curriculum resources, instructional approaches, and learning materials to help students understand concepts before progressing. This individualized approach allows us to meet students where they are while continually encouraging meaningful academic growth.

What is tuition?

Current annual tuition is:

Kindergarten through Grade 8: $6,166 per student
Grades 9 through 12: $8,400 per student

We are committed to keeping Christian education as accessible to families as possible. Scholarship opportunities may significantly reduce the amount a family is responsible for paying.

How can tuition be paid?

Families have flexibility in how they pay any tuition balance for which they are responsible. Tuition may be paid annually, semi-annually, quarterly, or monthly.

Our goal is to provide families with a payment schedule that makes planning for Christian education as manageable as possible.

What if our family cannot afford tuition?

Do not assume Christian education is out of reach based on the listed tuition amount alone.

We encourage eligible families to apply for Ohio's EdChoice Expansion Scholarship, which can provide scholarship funding toward private school tuition. Eligibility and scholarship amounts are determined according to current State of Ohio requirements.

Families interested in CSA are encouraged to contact us so we can discuss tuition, available scholarship opportunities, and the enrollment process with you.

When does the school year begin?

CSA begins its school year in July with a three-week Summer Camp for our 2-12 students. Following Summer Camp, students have a summer break before returning for the regular school schedule after Labor Day.

Our calendar differs from the traditional public-school calendar, so prospective families are encouraged to review the current CSA school calendar for specific dates.

Do you offer team sports?

CSA does not sponsor interscholastic athletic teams. Instead, families who want their students to participate in organized sports are encouraged to explore the many opportunities available through local community organizations, the YMCA, churches, and other area programs.

This allows students to participate in athletics and activities that interest them while CSA focuses its resources on our educational programs and school community.
